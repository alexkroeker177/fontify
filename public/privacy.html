Privacy Policy

The short version

Fontify runs entirely in your browser. Your handwriting, your drawings, and your generated fonts never leave your device. There are no accounts, no cookies, and no tracking of individual users.

Controller

The site operator's full details are currently being completed. Contact: GitHub.

Your drawings

Everything you draw is stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device, so you don't lose your work between visits. It is never transmitted to us. You can delete it at any time via "Start over" or by clearing your browser data.

Server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes your IP address in technical server logs to deliver the site and defend against abuse (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to count page views and anonymous product events (such as how often a font is downloaded). It sets no cookies, stores no personal data, and does not track you across sites (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - understanding aggregate usage of a free tool).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since we store no personal data about you beyond short-lived technical logs, there is usually nothing to hand over.
